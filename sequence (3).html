How to Play

  • Goal: Be first to form the required number of Sequences — five of your chips in a row, straight or diagonal.
  • Your turn: Play a card from your hand, place a chip on a matching space on the board, then draw a new card.
  • Two spaces each: Every card (except Jacks) appears twice on the board — pick whichever open spot suits your plan.
  • Two‑eyed Jacks (♦ ♣) are wild — place a chip on any open space.
  • One‑eyed Jacks (♥ ♠) let you remove one opponent chip that isn't already part of a finished Sequence.
  • Corners are free for everyone and count toward anyone's Sequence.
  • Dead card: if a card's two spaces are both taken, swap it for a new one — your turn continues.
  • Winning: 2 players need 2 Sequences. 3 players need just 1 Sequence.
